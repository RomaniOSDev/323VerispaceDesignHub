# Privacy Policy

This application helps you design and organize custom digital brush strokes for creative work.

## Data Collection

We store brush settings, usage statistics, and app preferences locally on your device using UserDefaults. No personal account is required.

## Analytics

Usage counters and achievement progress remain on your device and are not transmitted to external servers by this app.

## Third-Party Links

The Settings screen may open external web pages for privacy and terms information. Those sites have their own policies.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options provided on our privacy web page.
